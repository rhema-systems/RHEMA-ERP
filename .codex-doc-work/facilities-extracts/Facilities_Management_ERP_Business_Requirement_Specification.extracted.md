[1] (Heading 1) FACILITIES MANAGEMENT SECTION- ERP
BUSINESS REQUIREMENT SPECIFICATION (BRS)
[3] (Heading 2) 1. Purpose of Document
[4] (Normal) This document defines the business processes, functional requirements, user roles, and system capabilities required to develop a Facilities Management ERP module. The system will centralise lease management, maintenance operations, customer management, financial processes, asset management and reporting.
[5] (Heading 2) 2. ERP Modules Overview
[6] (Normal) Core modules required:
- User Management
- Property and Site Management
- Lease Management
- Maintenance Management
- Complaint Management
- Service Provider Management
- Staff/Cleaner Management
- Inventory Management
- Financial Management
- Budget Management
- Asset Register
- Document Management
- Reporting
[7] (Heading 2) 3. User Roles and Access
[8] (Normal) Facilities Manager/Supervisor: approvals, monitoring, reporting and administration.
Facility Officer: daily operations, leases, complaints, maintenance and billing.
Finance Officer: payment confirmation and financial records.
Service Provider: assigned jobs and invoice submission.
Customer/Tenant: complaints, documents and lease interactions.
[9] (Heading 2) 4. User Management Requirements
[10] (Normal) The system shall allow users to register, login, recover passwords, change passwords, update profiles and access features based on assigned roles.
[11] (Heading 2) 5. Property and Site Management Process Flow
[12] (Normal) Add Site → Create Property Record → Capture Units → Assign Occupancy Status → Monitor Availability.

Required fields: Site name, location, property type, unit details, responsible officer and occupancy status.
[13] (Heading 2) 6. Lease Management Process Flow
[14] (Normal) Prospective Client Registration → Property Selection → Interest Confirmation → Proforma Invoice → Lease Agreement Preparation → Electronic Signing → Payment Confirmation → Property Allocation → Lease Monitoring.

Required lease data: tenant details, lease dates, rental value, renewal date, termination date and status.
System alerts required for lease expiry and renewals.
[15] (Heading 2) 7. Maintenance Management Process Flow
[16] (Normal) Maintenance Request → Issue Logging → Priority Assignment → Resource Allocation → Service Provider Assignment → Completion → Inspection → Closure.

System must track preventive and corrective maintenance activities.
[17] (Heading 2) 8. Complaint Management
[18] (Normal) Complaint Submission → Registration → Assignment → Investigation → Resolution → Closure.
Required features: complaint tracking, escalation, history and reports.
[19] (Heading 2) 9. Service Provider Management
[20] (Normal) Maintain contractor database including company details, service category, contracts, rates, performance records and invoice history.
[21] (Heading 2) 10. Financial Management
[22] (Normal) Client Billing: Generate invoice → Send invoice → Receive payment → Update records.

Contractor Payments: Receive invoice → Verify work → Approve → Process payment.

System should capture invoices, taxes, payments and financial documents.
[23] (Heading 2) 11. Budget and Expense Management
[24] (Normal) Create Budget → Allocate Resources → Capture Expenses → Compare Actual vs Budget.
Required fields include budget year, department, planned amount, actual expense and variance.
[25] (Heading 2) 12. Asset Management
[26] (Normal) Maintain asset register including asset number, description, location, cost, warranty, maintenance history and current status.
[27] (Heading 2) 13. Document Management
[28] (Normal) System must support uploading, storing, searching and retrieving lease agreements, contracts, certificates, invoices, payment records and property documents.
[29] (Heading 2) 14. Reporting Requirements
[30] (Normal) Reports required:
- Lease reports
- Occupancy reports
- Maintenance reports
- Complaint reports
- Contractor reports
- Financial reports
- Inventory reports

Reports should export to PDF and Excel/CSV formats.
[31] (Heading 2) 15. Non-Functional Requirements
[32] (Normal) The ERP should provide:
- User friendly dashboard
- Fast search capability
- Notifications and reminders
- Audit trail
- Secure document storage
- Mobile responsive access
- Role-based security
[33] (Heading 2) 16. High Level Process Flow
[34] (Normal) Customer/Tenant → Lease Management → Billing → Payment Confirmation → Facility Operations → Maintenance/Complaints → Service Providers → Invoice Processing → Reports → Management Decisions
[35] (Heading 2) 17. Recommended Enhancements
[36] (Normal) Future improvements should include mobile application support, QR asset tracking, SLA monitoring, vendor ratings, automated approvals and integration with accounting systems.
