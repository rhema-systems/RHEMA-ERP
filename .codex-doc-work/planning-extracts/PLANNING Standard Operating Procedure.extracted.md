[1] (Normal) STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) OF THE PLANNING SECTION
[2] (Normal) The following are current Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) of the Town Planning Section of Development Department:

## Table 1
| No. | Activity/Task | Objective | Procedure |
| 1 | Vetting of applications for land allocations or temporal license | To ensure that application for parcel of land allocations or license aligns with approved master plan and TDC Land use strategy | Application/File is referred to Head of Development (HOD) / Application/File is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD with any supporting documents. / STP vets, and or refers the application to any Town Planner, or any Physical Planner or any other technical of the Section. / Application is subjected to internal technical review, cross reference of proposed land use with the approved master plan/layout. / Site visitation for situational report. / STP refers to the vetted application, whether it is recommended or rejected with justification to HOD for further action. |
| 2 | Change of Use Review | To ensure that the proposed change of use aligns with spatial development strategy and avoids land use conflicts. | Application/File is referred to Head of Development (HOD) / Application/File is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD with any supporting documents. / Application is referred to the change of use committee (HOD). / Application is subjected to the Committee’s vetting of proposed land use to determine if the proposed use is permissible or otherwise. / Site is visited for site report. / Stakeholder/neighborhood consultation is recommended and conducted if needed. / STP vets, review and or refers the application to any Town Planner, or any Physical Planner or any other technical of the Section. / STP refers to the vetted application, whether it is recommended or rejected with justification to HOD (Chairman of Committee). |
| 3 | Preparation of Planning Scheme (Layout) | To prepare any planning scheme and layout on any new acquisition by the Company. | HOD refers the any base map/site of the parcel under interest to STP / STP together with technical officers of the section (any Town Planner, any Physical Planner, DOS, etc) initiate internal process to prepare and design the layout. / The layout and all accompanied reports are produced. / The final layouts and all reports is forwarded to HOD for approval further action |
| 4 | Site Report | To visit site and report on ground situation on any requested issue/file/application. | Application/File/Letter is referred to Head of Development (HOD) / Application/File is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD with any supporting documents. / STP visit site, and or refers the application to any Town Planner, or any Physical Planner any other technical of the Section for site visit / The assigned Officer visit site and a site report is produced / The report is forwarded to STP, and STP refers |
| 5 | Preparation of Site Plan | Prepare site plan for lessees/transferees and verify that the site plans meet spatial and planning standards | Application/File is referred to Head of Development (HOD) / Application/File is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD with any supporting documents. / STP vets, and or refers the application to any Town Planner, or any Physical Planner any other technical of the Section. / File is referred to Drawing Office Supervisor (DOS) to prepare Site plan. / DOS prepare or refer file to any draughtsman to prepare site plan. / DOS review plot dimensions, road access, utility corridor, buffer, easement, validation of survey coordinates. / DOS refer the file to any Draughtman for site plan preparation / Site plan is prepared, approved, or amended, and signed by draughtman, DOS and STP / Application/File is referred to Head of Development (HOD) |
| 6 | Official Search and Provision of Data | To provide verified land use information based on available records to client | Application/File is referred to and received by STP from HOD with any supporting documents such as consent for search. / Site plan attached to the application is subjected to internal search for prepared site plans, records in notebooks, for check. / Attached site plan is manually superimposed on existing layout or prepared site plans. / The section confirms whether there are available records or not and recommends Estate and Revenue to also check their records. / Information on the land status is provided with attached site plan, zoning status, or any other information to HOD for further action. / Application/File is referred to HOD with the information from Planning attached. |
| 7 | Development Permit Conformity Review | To assess whether development proposal conforms with the | Site plan is checked with the layout to check land use compatibility / The land use for the proposed development is also checked example for height zoning of the area, etc. / Inconsistencies, request for revision is advised if necessary  / Approve and endorse for further processing / File is referred |
| 8 | Undertake Regularization | To guide the regularization of unplanned or informally occupied TDC lands | Process 1 / Application/File is referred to and received by STP from HOD on any numbered parcel/plot. / The section checks and verify whether there is existing record on the parcel/plot under interest. / The layout is checked and cross-referenced against what is existing on the ground and layout  / The section conducts site verification and area profiling with a site report. / Site plan is prepared if there the application is favorable. / Application/File is referred to HOD for further action. / Process 2 / The Section receives a application letter from Regularization Office. / The section checks and verify whether there is existing record on the parcel/plot under interest. / The site plan is checked and cross-referenced against what is existing layout  / STP minutes any available information to the regularization office for any further action. / The regularization committee undertakes site visit if there are no records. / A site report is prepared by the regularization committee to Managing Director (MD) for approval or disapproval. / When approved, the application is referred to regularization committee. / The application is referred to HOE and HOD, and HOD will refer the application to STP to prepare Site Plan. / DOS prepare or refer file to any draughtsman to prepare site plan. / DOS review plot dimensions, road access, utility corridor, buffer, easement, validation of survey coordinates. / DOS refer the file to any Draughtman for site plan preparation / Site plan is prepared, approved, or amended, and signed by draughtsman, DOS and STP / Application/File is referred to Head of Development (HOD) |
| 9 | Layout Review and Correction | To identify and rectify anomalies in the approved layout. | Based on audit findings/recommendations anomalies on the layout is identified and rectified / The issues raised are subjected to internal technical review, cross reference of proposed land use with the approved master plan/layout. / Where files are required to correct or review, the section officially request the files from Estate Department. / STP refers the files to DOS to correct all anomaly to amend or update the layout. / STP vets and response and updated layout is submitted to HOD for further action. |
| 10 | Compliance Site Inspection and Reporting | To physically inspect and verify on ground situation | Application/File/Letter is referred to Head of Development (HOD) / Application/File/Letter is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD with any supporting documents. / STP vets, review and or refers the application to any Town Planner, or any Physical Planner or any other technical of the Section. / Site inspection is conducted and a site report is produced. / STP refers the site report to HOD for further action |
| 11 | Dispute Resolution and Client Complaint Management | To handle planning related complaints and boundary dispute fairly | Application/File/Letter is referred to Head of Development (HOD) / Application/File/Letter is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD with any supporting documents. / STP vets, review and or refers the application to any Town Planner, or any Physical Planner any other technical of the Section. / The issue is subjected to internal review or assessment. / STP reports and liase with HOD for further action based on available records from the Section. |
| 12 | District Assembly Spatial Planning Committee Meetings | To represent TDC’s interest in the MMDAs permitting process | Invitation letter is referred to and received by Supervising Town Planner (STP) from HOD. / STP attends the meeting or assigns any Town Planner or Physical Planner, or an Officer of the Section to attend the meeting. / The assigned officer after the meeting prepares a report and submits it to HOD through STP for further action. |
