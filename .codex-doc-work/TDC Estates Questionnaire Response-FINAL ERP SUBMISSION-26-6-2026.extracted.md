4. 1. Purpose of this Workshop Pack

5. This questionnaire is designed to support requirement gathering for the ERP Land Management & Acquisition, Property Management, Estate Management, and Home Ownership Scheme modules to be implemented for TDC Ghana Ltd as a State-Owned Enterprise. It is tailored for TDC’s mixed operating model as a public land developer, serviced-plot and site-and-services provider, housing and estate developer/manager, and participant in home ownership and affordable housing delivery. It is intended for workshops, interviews, and stakeholder consultations to capture current land administration processes, acquisition workflows, property and tenancy operations, estate services, customer allocation processes, home ownership scheme administration, reporting needs, integrations, and future-state expectations.

6. The completed questionnaire will be used to validate business needs, define scope, support solution design, and identify priorities for configuration, data migration, testing, and change management. For TDC, the questionnaire should reflect the practical reality of managing a large public land bank and long-term leasehold land area, serviced plots and site-and-services schemes, housing estates, rental and property-service operations, and mixed homeownership products including outright sale, installment-based purchase, and rent-to-own or public-worker housing partnership scenarios. Public information indicates that TDC Ghana Ltd was established to plan and develop approximately 63 square miles of acquisition land in the Tema area and now operates with a broader national real-estate development mandate, including serviced plots, housing projects, estates, and customer-facing services. State Interests and Governance Authority, the Ministry of Works, Housing and Water Resources, and the National Homeownership Fund show that TDC is relevant to public land development, estate delivery, and affordable/homeownership partnerships. Recent TDC Ghana Ltd communications also highlight customer-service improvements such as a 24-hour call centre and ongoing housing and serviced-land offerings.
