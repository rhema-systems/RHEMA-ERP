12. SYNOPSIS OF THE LEGAL DEPARTMENT
13. The legal department is responsible for ensuring that the company operates within the confines of the law as well as adhering to regulatory requirements and industry best practices.
14. THE DEPARTMENT ‘S STAFF
15. PREPARATION OF LEASES, SUPPLEMENTARY LEASES, DEED OF VARIATION
16. Procedure
17. The Legal department receives the property file from the Estate department with a request for drawing up a lease addressed to the Head of Legal Services.
18. The file is reviewed (due diligence) to ascertain whether payments have been made and to confirm whether cadastral plans and schedule have been inserted.
19. The property file is then sent for typing of a draft lease to be reviewed and approved.
20. The Lessee is invited witness his/her witness to sign the Lease.
21. The lease is sealed and referred to the Head of Legal for signature and for onward transmission to the Managing Director for signature.
22. The Lessee is then called to pick up the lease at the Legal department for registration at the Lands Commission, Accra.
23. PREPARATION OF CONTENTS TO ASSIGN & RECOGNITION OF VESTING
24. Procedure
25. The Legal department receives the property file from the Estate department addressed to the Head of Legal Services.
26. The files is for review (due diligence) of the property file to ascertain whether payments have been made and to confirm whether cadastral plans and schedule have been inserted.
27. Referral of files to Administrative Assistants for preparation of draft Consents to assign, and recognition of vesting.
28. The legal officers vet the drafts Consents to assign, and recognition of vesting.
29. Final printing of consents on a letterhead and referred to the Head of Legal for signature and for onward transmission to the Managing Director for signature.
30. The consent is then dated and sealed.
31. The Lessee is then called to pick up the consent from the legal department for registration at the Lands Commission, Accra.
32. The property file is then sent back to the estate department.
33. PREPARATION OF CONTENTS TO MORTGAGE
34. Procedure
35. The Legal department receives the property file from the Estate department addressed to the Head of Legal Services.
36. The files are then distributed to the Legal Officers for review (due diligence) of the property file.
37. Referral of files to Administrative Assistants for preparation of draft Consents to mortgage.
38. The legal officers vet the drafts Consents to mortgage.
39. Final printing of consents on a letterhead and referred to the Head of Legal for signature and for onward transmission to the Managing Director for signature.
40. The consent is then dated and sealed.
41. The Lessee is then called to pick up the consent from the legal department.
42. The property file is then sent back to the estate department.
43. PREPARATION OF TRANSFERS
44. Procedure
45. Due diligence on files by legal officers
46. Applicants are invited together with the transferees for interviews.
47. File referred to the estate department for calculation of requisite fees for approval by the Managing Director
48. File is approved and received by the legal department from the Managing Directors office.
49. The file is referred for requisite fees to be paid.
50. After payment transfer declaration is prepared for execution by the parties with their witnesses, legal clerks and legal officers.
51. The file is referred to Head of Legal for signature.
52. Copies of the signed transfer forms are distributed to the applicants and a copy placed on file.
53. The file is referred to Head of Legal for onward transmission to the estate department for amendment of records.
54. TERMINATION OF & RECOGNITION OF TENANCY
55. Procedure
56. Due diligence on files by legal officers
57. Review of site report and referral to Legal Clerks for preparation of draft termination of tenancy letters legal clerks.
58. The letter is vetted by the legal officers and referred for signing by Head of Legal.
59. The notice of termination is pasted on the affected properties for 21 days by the legal clerk.
60. After the expiration of the notice, payment of the requisite fees and drafting of tenancy declaration and review by the legal officers.
61. Tenancy declaration is prepared for execution by the parties with their witnesses, signed legal clerks and legal officers.
62. The file is referred to Head of Legal for signature.
63. Copies of the signed transfer forms are distributed to the applicants and a copy placed on file.
64. The file is referred to Head of Legal for onward transmission to the estate department for amendment of records.
65. RECEIVING AND FILING OF COURT PROCESSES
66. Procedure
67. WRIT OF SUMMONS
68. Writ is served on the company and received by the Legal Clerks.
69. A docket is opened and writ filed and sent to Head of Legal for assignment.
70. Appearance is prepared and filed by the Legal clerk.
71. A request is made for property file and for attachment to court docket.
72. Legal officer then prepares other court processes in defense of the company.
73. OTHER COURT PROCESS
74. Court process is served on the company and received by the Legal Clerks.
75. The bailiff endorses the back of the court process and same is done by the legal clerk in the dispatch book of the bailiff.
76. Court process is recorded and sent to the assigned Lawyer.
77. Lawyer receives and files court process on case docket.
78. Legal officer then prepares other court processes in defense of the company
