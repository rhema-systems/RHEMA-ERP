# Finance producer-intent groups (C8)

C8 adds a Finance-owned zero-or-all boundary for an ordered set of distinct C7 producer intents. It exists for owner operations such as Inventory disposal that have more than one genuine source identity and economic meaning. Each member remains a normal C6 `AccountingEvent`, retains its source/action/idempotency and correction/reversal lineage, and freezes its own C5 calculation, selection and ordered-book evidence. The group never combines those identities and never lets a producer enumerate or choose books.

One maker prepares the entire canonical ordered member set. One different checker approves or rejects the complete immutable group with a governed reason; members cannot be approved separately or silently dropped. Exact retries return the durable group, while any change to member payload, identity, order, lineage, maker, checker, reason or owner-effect evidence fails closed. Group corrections and reversals preserve member order and target every predecessor member, so every member executes against its target's original frozen book set despite later C5 policy drift or blockers.

Execution has no HTTP endpoint. A reviewed owner service opens a `Serializable` transaction on the same scoped `ApplicationDbContext`, stages its database mutation, and calls the internal data-only C8 method with one deterministic receipt bound to the full group. Finance neither accepts executable callbacks nor starts, commits or rolls back that transaction. It verifies the ambient transaction identity before and after every member, records the group receipt, and invokes existing C6 authority for every member. Any exception is returned to the owner so the outer transaction rolls back all owner, C5, C6 and receipt writes. Only after `CurrentTransaction` is null may the separate reviewed failure method clear EF tracking and persist durable group/member failure evidence.

`20260908120000_AddProducerIntentGroupsC8` is additive and unapplied. It refuses an absent or partial C7 base, creates tenant-bound immutable group/member/receipt/attempt evidence, enforces cardinality, identity, lineage, maker/checker, workflow, exclusivity and outcome constraints, and permits a C7-pending member to enter C6 only under a complete approved group with its exact receipt. Its `Down` refuses to discard any group evidence and restores the C7 event trigger only when all C8 tables are empty. C6, C7 and C8 remain disabled by default; C1 direct-leaf rejection and all C2-C6 accounting authority are unchanged.
