1. Leave

"Leave is the transaction everybody in the room has raised, approved or chased. Watch three people: Efua raises it, her Head of Development decides it, HR sees the balance move — and nobody types a status."

1.1 The employee raises a request

staff

Time, Leave & Pay → My Leave · /me/leave

Balances at the top: Annual 21 days (with days taken and remaining), Sick 12, Casual 6. Below, her requests: LV2026000001 annual leave next week, Approved; LV2026000002 annual leave from 23 September, Pending.
1New request/me/leave/new. Choose Casual Leave, one day next month, reason "Passport renewal". Notice the working-day count excludes the weekend and the public holiday calendar (Book 2 §1.5).
2RelieverPick a colleague as reliever. Say: "A reliever must be an active employee, cannot be on leave the same days, and is told. The system checks all three."
3SubmitThe request appears as Pending with a number. Point out she cannot approve it herself — there is no button.
4Planner/me/leave/planner: her December plan, ten days, with a reliever. Say: "Plans are declared before the year; HR sees clashes across a unit before anyone is refused."

1.2 The manager decides

head.dev

Human Resources → Time & Leave → Leave Management → Approvals · /hr/leave/approvals

His queue: Efua's two pending requests and the casual leave just raised. Each row: who, type, dates, days, reliever, balance after.
1ApproveOpen the casual leave, Approve with a comment. Status → Approved.
2Reject oneOpen LV2026000002 (23 September), Reject with "Clashes with the year-end valuation; please re-apply for November". Say: "A rejection reason is mandatory and the employee sees it verbatim."
3The employee sees itBack in staff, refresh /me/leave: one Approved, one Rejected with the reason, and the annual balance unchanged by the rejection.
Approval is a workflow step on the engine; the approver is whoever the definition names (line manager or HR) and who passes the record's own rule — the request's employee's manager, never the employee. The balance is decremented by the approval, not by the request, so a rejected request never touches it.

1.3 HR's registers

hr.head
1Requests/hr/leave/requests: twelve requests across the Corporation, filterable by unit, type, status, dates.
2Balances/hr/leave/balances: every employee's balance per type for the year, with entitlement, taken, pending, remaining, carried over.
3Adjustments/hr/leave/adjustments: the opening entitlements were loaded here (303 rows). Add one live: pick an employee, Annual, +2 days, reason "Recalled from leave — days restored". Say: "Every manual movement of a balance is a signed adjustment with a reason code; nobody edits a balance."
4Encashments/hr/leave/encashments: converting unused leave to pay at a rate derived from emoluments, approved and then handed to payroll.
5Compliance/hr/leave/compliance: who has not taken their mandatory annual leave — the "use it or lose it" rule per leave type.
6Year-end/hr/leave/year-end. Show, do not click. Carry-over up to the type's maximum, then forfeiture, run once at year end with a preview of every balance it will change.

1.4 Leave types and policy

Administration → HR → Leave Types · /administration/hr/leave-types

Nine types: Annual, Sick, Maternity, Paternity, Compassionate, Casual, Study, Leave of Absence (unpaid), Occupational Injury. Open Annual: 21 days, 14 days' notice, reliever required, weekends and holidays not counted, carry-over 5 days expiring after 3 months, mandatory, accessible after 12 months' service, encashable.
"Every rule you saw enforced is a setting on this screen — nothing is in code. The Labour Act's floors are the starting point; TDC's own policy replaces them here."

Click Edit: the zone is drawn on a map — drag the pin, slide the radius, or switch the shape to Polygon and click the corners of the compound. Say: "HR draws the fence on the map or stands at the gate and presses Use my position; a zone can warn or refuse, and it is linked to the work location so it follows the staff posted there."
Punch → log → daily attendance → monthly summary → payroll export is a pipeline with a lock at the summary; nothing after the lock can change what was exported. Biometric devices sync into the same log table, so a device is just another source of punches — and a device proves location by where it is bolted, so the zone check is for phones, not readers.

Per-diem rates and exchange rates come from Finance's currency tables; a claim converts at the published rate and records which rate it used. Advances, settlements and unpaid approved claims are the Finance hand-off — registered for the GL posting sweep, not posted from HR.

Ten assets: four laptops (one a CAD workstation), two handsets, two pool vehicles (Toyota Hilux GT 4412-21, Nissan Navara GT 2210-22), a Leica total station and a Trimble GNSS receiver. Eight are assigned.

3Transfers, returns, surchargesTransfer between holders (approved), return on separation, and a surcharge when an item is lost or damaged — the surcharge feeds payroll deductions (/hr/assets/payroll).

5. Medical and health

hr.head

Human Resources → Medical & Health → Dashboard · /hr/medical/dashboard

Claims by status and month, insurance utilisation, upcoming appointments, facilities and physicians.
1Facilities and physicians/hr/medical/facilities: four healthcare facilities with services and NHIS acceptance; /hr/medical/physicians: accredited physicians and their verification.
2Insurance/hr/medical/insurance: providers, plans, employee policies with dependants, premiums, provider documents.
3Claims/hr/medical/claims: expense claims through create → approve → pay, with pre-authorisation where the scheme requires it. Open a paid one and show the approval trail.
4NHIS/hr/medical/nhis: NHIS claims with their documents.
5Clinical/hr/medical/clinical: referrals and appointments; /hr/medical/health: health profiles, conditions, allergies, medical examinations.
6The employee's sidestaff: Health & Safety → My Medical · /me/medical: her claims and appointments, and where her cover and health profile appear once HR issues a policy and the clinic records a profile (she has neither yet — say so; it is the honest state of a new file). Say: "She files her own claim; HR does not file for her."
Attaching a receipt to a medical claim, or any other file in this book, goes through the controlled upload gate, where a clean malware scan is mandatory. A refusal reading FILE_VIRUS_SCAN_INCOMPLETE means the document scanner is not running — Book 0 §3.2.

Occupational health — surveillance, first aid, wellness, return to work — is owned by SHE (Book 4 §10) and bridges to these records by reference. Both are gated by the medical permission family so a SHE officer sees what a nurse would, not what HR would.

6. Benefits and emoluments

hr.head
1Emoluments/hr/emoluments: six pay components (basic, and allowances such as housing, transport, responsibility) with tax treatment, pension applicability and effective dating; position emoluments attach them to positions by grade.
2Benefit policies/administration/hr/compensation/benefit-policies: ten policies — Medical Cover (Standard), Life Insurance (Basic), Transport and Housing Allowances, Education Support, Technology Stipend, and the benefits-in-kind (company vehicle with fuel and driver, furnished accommodation, responsibility allowance, tax-exempt medical reimbursement) with the GRA valuation rates.
3Enrolments/hr/benefits: 45 enrolments — medical cover for confirmed staff, life cover for M1–M3, company vehicles for the Managing Director and the two General Managers.
4The refusalTry to enrol new.hire Kojo Ansah on Medical Cover (Standard). Refused: "probation rule". Say: "The policy is post-confirmation; the system knows he is on probation. When his confirmation is recorded in Book 1 §7, this enrolment goes through."
Payroll owns the pay-component master; HR keeps pension, tax treatment, gross-pay effect and effective dating alongside it. Salary grades are payroll's, mirrored into HR read-only. This is why there is no HR grade editor. Placement on a grade and notch is only possible for staff who are on payroll (Book 1 §1): the four off-payroll people cannot be placed, and an emolument summary for them reads a basic pay of zero — nothing from payroll, by design.
