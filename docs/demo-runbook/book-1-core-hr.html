The photograph and every attachment are stored through the central document-management gate (FileUploadRecord → DocumentRecord → version), which scans, types and audits each file. A clean malware scan is mandatory for all 29 HR upload categories and a tenant administrator cannot opt out of it — an upload with no clean result is refused outright. The legacy "picture path" field is retained for imported values only.

On this laptop the scanner is a stub (Book 0 §3.2) so the flow can be shown. Do not tell the room files are being scanned here; Book 0 §5 has the accurate wording if anyone asks.

Say: "HR says who should be paid through the run; Payroll decides who is. This screen is where the two are checked against each other."

46 job descriptions. Open any one: purpose, key responsibilities, qualifications, required competencies, working conditions, the position(s) it covers, version and approval status.
1Coverage gapsPositions with no current job description. Say: "A position without a job description cannot be advertised — the vacancy pulls its advert text from here."
2Job families and levelsAdministration → HR → Job Positions: families, sub-families and career levels the descriptions are classified against.
3Competencies/administration/hr/competencies: six framework competencies (CAD & drawing production, Physical planning, Project coordination, Communication, Leading a team, Safety awareness) on a 1–5 scale. Book 3 §3 shows them assessed.

The detail read loads them as separate queries — the first version materialised their Cartesian product and timed out at 46 descriptions. Worth mentioning to a technical audience as an example of the kind of defect found by using seeded data at realistic volume.

5. Recruitment — end to end

hr.head

Human Resources → Recruitment · dashboard /hr/recruitment/dashboard

"Recruitment runs from an established vacancy to a person on the register, and every step is a record: requisition, vacancy, advert, candidate, application, interview, offer, hire. Nothing skips a step, and the person who is finally created carries the hire record that created them."

7. Probation and confirmation

hr.head

Human Resources → Probation & Confirmation → Register · /hr/probation

Six probation periods, one per new starter, each with a scheduled first review three months from the hire date and a confirming authority.
"Probation is not a status somebody remembers to change. The period is created from the contract, the reviews are scheduled, the reviewer is the line manager, and confirmation is a decision by the confirming authority that writes the confirmation date onto the employee and unlocks the benefits that were withheld until then."
1Open Kojo Ansah's periodHired 15 July; review 1 scheduled for mid-October; reviewer Kwasi Danquah; second reviewer HR.
2Reviews to conductSwitch to head.dev: /hr/probation/reviews. Two of his people — Patrick Appiah and Kojo Ansah. Open one and show the review form: ratings, comments, recommendation (confirm / extend / terminate).
3The employee's sideSwitch to new.hire: Career & Jobs → My Probation · /me/probation. His own period, reviews and their outcomes.
4Oath of secrecy/me/oath: he affirms the oath himself — date, IP address and the text version are recorded. HR's register at /hr/probation/oaths.
5Confirming authoritiesIn the admin window (this is Administration-tier configuration, not an HR screen): /administration/hr/probation/confirming-authorities — which role confirms which grade band. And Reminders: the sweep that warns HR and the reviewer before a review falls due, with its run log.
Confirmation is refused if any scheduled review is unconducted, and a benefit policy marked "post-confirmation" refuses enrolment for anyone still on probation (Book 2 §6 shows the refusal message). The two rules are the reason the six starters are visible on the benefits screen as "withheld", not "missing".
