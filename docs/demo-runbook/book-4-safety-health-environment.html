Book 4 — Safety, Health & Environment

From a near-miss reported on a phone to a closed corrective action, a signed permit to work, a PPE issue, a contractor induction, an environmental permit and the monthly report. Roughly 50 minutes performed in full.

A note on the sample dataThe seeded incidents and hazards use generic industrial narratives (a conveyor pinch point, a forklift, welding fumes). Present them as illustrative: "these are sample records; TDC's own will read like a building site and an estate." Everything you create live will be TDC's.

1. The dashboard

she.officer

Safety (SHE) → Dashboard (its own sidebar item, directly beneath Human Resources) · /hr/safety/dashboard

Tiles: open incidents, overdue corrective actions, hazards due for review, high-risk hazards, risk assessments expiring, inspections due, permits active / expiring / suspended, equipment due for inspection or with expiring certification, PPE below re-order. Every tile is a number today, and every number is a link.
"Safety is run from this screen. Each tile is a question the SHE officer would otherwise ask by walking round — what is open, what is overdue, what expires this month — and each answer is a list she can act on. The dashboard is not a report; it is the to-do list."

2. Incidents

Safety (SHE) → Incidents & Hazards → Incidents · /hr/safety/incidents

Four: INC-2026-001 (a hand caught at a pinch point — lost-time injury, under investigation), INC-2026-002 (a dropped load, a near-miss — reported), INC-2026-003 (a small electrical fire — corrective actions in progress), INC-2026-004 (a slip, first aid only — closed). Each reported by a named TDC employee.
1Open INC-2026-001Walk the tabs: the report (what, where, when, who, witnesses, injured body parts, injury type); investigation team and root cause; corrective actions with owners and due dates; documents and photographs; follow-ups; the statutory submission to the regulator with its reference; the link to an insurance claim.
2Report one liveSwitch to staff: Health & Safety → My Safety → Report · /me/safety/report/incident. "Loose paving slab at the Community 25 site office entrance; a visitor stumbled, no injury." Location, date, type Near-miss. Submit. Say: "Any employee reports from their phone; nothing needs an officer to be present."
3Triage itBack as she.officer: the new incident is at the top of the register. Classify it, assign an investigator, add a corrective action ("Re-lay the slab; barrier the entrance until done") with an owner — Kwasi Danquah — and a due date next week.
4The owner closes itSwitch to head.dev: Safety (SHE) → Incidents & Hazards → Corrective Actions · /hr/safety/corrective-actions: the action is his. Mark it complete with a note and evidence. Back on the dashboard the open-actions tile drops by one.
Reporting is open to every employee (/me/safety), and an anonymous route exists for environmental incidents. Reading, investigating and closing are gated by the SHE permission family. Statutory reporting to the Department of Factories Inspectorate is a record on the incident with the body, the date and the reference — the reminder engine chases the deadline.

5. Permits to work

Safety (SHE) → Inspections & Audits → Permits to Work · /hr/safety/permits

Three permits — PTW-2026-001 and PTW-2026-002 active, PTW-2026-003 expired — each for a type of work (hot work, confined space, work at height, electrical), a location, a window of validity, the named workers, the isolations and precautions, the issuer and the acceptor, extensions and closure.
"Nobody does hot work or enters a confined space on a TDC site without a permit. The permit says who, where, until when, what has been isolated, and who signed it on and off. When it expires the work stops — the dashboard shows expiring permits three days out."
1Open an active permitWorkers, precautions checklist, gas test if required, the extension history, the document attached.
2Issue one live/hr/safety/permits/new: hot work, "Welding of handrails, Community 24 office", tomorrow 08:00–16:00, two named workers, issuer she.officer. Save. It appears as Active with its number.
3Suspend itOpen it, Suspend with a reason ("Rain — cover removed"). The dashboard's suspended-permits tile shows one.

8. Safety training

Safety (SHE) → People & Health → Safety Training · /hr/safety/training

Programmes (induction, fire warden, first aid, working at height), plans and attendances — for employees and contractors' staff — with certificate expiry against the statutory requirement.
"This is deliberately separate from the corporate training you saw in Book 3. Safety training has to record people who are not employees — a contractor's welder — and it has to say when a certificate lapses under the regulation. Neither fits a learning-and-development catalogue."

Safety (SHE) → People & Health → Occupational Health · /hr/safety/occupational-health · Return to Work · /hr/safety/return-to-work

Health surveillance programmes (noise, dust, drivers' eyesight), first-aid stations and their first-aiders, wellness programmes; return-to-work plans after injury or long absence, with phases, adjustments and reviews.
1SurveillanceWho is enrolled in which surveillance programme, when last examined, when next due.
2Return to workOpen a plan: the phased return (hours, duties), the adjustments, the reviews with the medical officer, the sign-off. Say: "The injury in INC-2026-001 will end here — a plan that brings the person back safely, tied to the incident and to the medical record."
These screens ride the medical permission family, not the SHE one, so a SHE officer sees what a nurse would see — the surveillance and the plan — and not the employee's wider medical file. The bridge to Medical (Book 2 §5) is by reference.

14. Configuration

admin

Administration → Safety (SHE) (its own entry since 2026-09-03; the SHE roles reach it without HR administration rights) · /administration/hr/safety

1Reference dataIncident types, injury types, body parts, corrective-action templates, regulatory bodies, PPE types, PPE requirements by position, inspection checklists.
2Reminder engine/administration/hr/safety/reminders: what is chased (permit expiry, equipment inspection, certificate expiry, statutory returns, action due dates), to whom, how far ahead; the run log. Show the preview; do not run it.
Any step here that attaches a file — evidence on a corrective action, a permit document, a controlled document, a contractor's insurance — goes through the same gate as the rest of HR, and a clean malware scan is mandatory. If an attach is refused with FILE_VIRUS_SCAN_INCOMPLETE, the document scanner is not running: Book 0 §3.2.

The SHE reference catalogues and the seeded sample dataset were shaped so that every dashboard tile reports a non-zero count on a fresh database; the tiles are the acceptance test. Occupational-health screens are gated by the medical policies; everything else by the SHE policies; and the two reporting routes an ordinary employee uses are open to the Employee role only.
