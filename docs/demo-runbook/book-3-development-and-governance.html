Book 3 — Development & Governance

How people grow and how the Corporation governs them: performance, training, competencies, succession, awards, movements, discipline, employee relations, separation — and the self-service portal that ties it together. Roughly 60 minutes performed in full.

1. Performance

hr.head

Human Resources → Performance → Appraisal Cycles · /hr/performance/cycles

The Annual Performance Cycle 2026, with its phase deadlines (goal setting, Q1, mid-year, Q3, self-evaluation, peer, manager, calibration, HR review) and the appraisal settings it runs under — self-evaluation weight, peer reviews, manager authority, calibration, appeals window.
"Performance is a year-long cycle, not a form in December. Goals are set and approved in the cycle, checked in on through the year, evaluated by the person, their peers and their manager with stated weights, calibrated across units, reviewed by HR, and only then does a score become a recommendation — and every one of those stages has a deadline the system enforces."

1.1 Goals

1Company goals/hr/performance/company-goals — the strategic goals for the year, cascading to Unit Goals and Team Goals.
2Employee goalsSwitch to staff: Performance → My Goals · /me/performance/goals. Three approved goals — Community 25 Phase 2 layout (40%), drawing-office turnaround (30%), Civil 3D certification (30%) — with progress bars. Update progress on one: 60%, with a note and evidence.
3The manager's viewSwitch to head.dev: /hr/performance/employee-goals filtered to his team; and Goals At Risk /hr/performance/at-risk — goals behind their expected progress by the threshold in Administration → HR → Performance → Goal Risk Thresholds.
A goal moves Draft → PendingApproval → Approved → (locked at the evaluation phase). Only the goal's owner can submit it; only their direct manager — checked against the employee record, never a supplied id — can approve, reject or lock it.

1.2 Check-ins, conversations, the journal

1Check-inhead.dev: Performance → Conversations · /hr/performance/conversations: his Q3 one-to-one with Efua, scheduled for this week, with an agenda. Open it and record notes and agreed actions.
2The journalstaff: /me/performance/journal: her running notes of achievements, kept by her, visible to her manager at evaluation.
3Interim reviews/hr/performance/interim-reviews: the quarterly and mid-year touchpoints the cycle schedules.

1.3 Appraisals, calibration, HR review, appeals

1Team appraisalshead.dev: /hr/performance/team-appraisals: his direct reports' 2026 appraisals with each stage's status.
2My appraisalstaff: /me/performance/appraisals: her 2026 appraisal — self-evaluation, peer nominations, the score breakdown she is allowed to see.
3Calibrationhr.head: /hr/performance/calibration: sessions that align ratings across units before HR review.
4HR review, recommendations, proposals/hr/performance/hr-review → /recommendations → /proposals: the outcome recommendation (increment, promotion, training, PIP) becomes a salary-review or employment-action proposal that goes to the Managing Director on the workflow.
5Appeals/hr/performance/appeals: within the appeals window an employee contests a score; the appeal is decided and its outcome recorded.
6Analytics and deadline enforcement/hr/performance/analytics and /hr/performance/deadline-enforcement: distribution of ratings, stage completion, who is late.
Scores are a three-level weighted mean — criteria within a section, sections within a template, and the self / peer / manager perspectives at the weights in the cycle's settings. Three peers with identical scores must produce the same result as one; the model was tested exactly that way.

1.4 Performance improvement plan

1The PIPhr.head: /hr/performance/pip: one plan for a member of the Development Department — late monthly reports and two missed inspections; expected standards, improvement actions, support (coaching, reduced load), measurement, fortnightly reviews with formal reviews at day 45 and 90.
2Meetings and outcomeOpen it: the review meetings tab records each meeting; the plan closes as met, extended or not met, and "not met" is the input to discipline (§7) or separation (§9).

Four plans for posts whose incumbents retire within three years: General Manager Finance & Administration, Head of Estates, Head of Sales & Marketing, Head of Task Force. Each: criticality, risk, the retirement and anticipated-vacancy dates, an emergency successor, and two ranked candidates with readiness (12 and 24 months), ratings, strengths, development gaps and retention risk.

6. Staff movements

hr.head

Human Resources → Staff Movements → Register · /hr/movements

A promotion of a Draughtsman (S1) to a vacant M5 post, effective in a month, submitted and awaiting approval; and an acting appointment — a Supervising officer acts as Head of Development during his leave, with a fixed acting allowance.

The establishment rule — refuse a movement into a post that is already at its expected headcount — is advisory rather than blocking today, because expected headcount is only maintained where a manpower budget has been approved for the unit.

7. Discipline

hr.head

Human Resources → Discipline → Cases · /hr/discipline

Two cases. DC-2026-00001 — persistent lateness, a Minor case: reported, submitted, a written query (show-cause) issued. DC-2026-00002 — negligence causing loss (a pool vehicle left unlocked; battery stolen, GHS 1,850): investigated, reviewed, written query issued, and a proposed decision — written warning and a 50% surcharge — awaiting confirmation.
"Discipline follows natural justice by construction. The system will not let a decision be recorded before the employee has been given a written query and the chance to answer it — that is a rule in the service, not a note in a manual. And a decision is proposed by HR but confirmed by an authority above."
1Open DC-2026-00002Offence, severity, incident and report dates, the investigation, the hearing, the notifications sent, the employee's response, the proposed decision with its rationale.
2Confirm the decisionSwitch to md.tdc: Discipline → Awaiting My Confirmation · /hr/discipline/approvals. Open it, confirm. The surcharge is raised for payroll recovery.
3Queueshr.head: /hr/discipline/queues: investigations and hearings due; the reminder sweep for overdue responses.
4The employee's sidestaff: /me/discipline: her own record — empty, which is the point; and the two subjects would see their case, the query, and where to answer.
5CatalogueAdministration → HR → Discipline → Catalogue: offences (absence, lateness, negligence, insubordination) and action types (verbal warning, written warning, suspension, surcharge).

8. Employee relations

staff

Career & Jobs → My Grievances · /me/grievances

GRV-2026-00001 — "Unequal allocation of site allowance in the drawing office", filed by Efua, at the Supervisor rung, answered by the Head of Development (the omission confirmed; arrears with October payroll), not yet resolved.
"A grievance climbs a ladder — supervisor, head of department, HR, the Managing Director — and at each rung a named person must answer within a set time. The statement is kept verbatim. The employee can escalate if the answer does not resolve it; nobody above can file it for her, and nobody can answer their own."
1Escalate or acceptAs staff, open it: read the response; either accept (resolves) or escalate to the next rung with a reason.
2The responder's sidehead.dev: Employee relations → Awaiting my response: his queue and response deadline.
3HR's registerhr.head: /hr/employee-relations: all cases, rungs, deadlines; /responders: who answers each rung for each unit; /analytics: cases by unit, subject and time-to-resolve.
4Anonymous concerns/hr/employee-relations/concerns, raised from /me/concerns without a name — HR sees the concern, never the sender.

9. Separation

hr.head

Human Resources → Separations & Exit → Register · /hr/separations

One separation: Godfred Gyasi, TDC/00028, voluntary resignation to join the Lands Commission, notice given, last working day in three weeks — Pending Approval, awaiting the Managing Director's signature.
1Sign itSwitch to md.tdc: open the separation, Approve. Say: "The Managing Director's signature is on every non-procedural separation — that is the authority on the record, not a permission HR could give itself."
2Clearancehr.head: the clearance checklist opens — assets to return (his laptop), loans, library, IT accounts, exit interview — each item cleared by its owning unit. The template is under Administration → HR → Separation → Clearance Form.
3SettlementThe final settlement: leave encashment, outstanding claims, deductions (an asset surcharge if any), notice-period adjustment. Submit it for review.
4Internal Audit reviewsSwitch to auditor: the settlement in his queue; review and pass it. Say: "Audit's review sits before the payment — a role that reports to the Board, on a record HR cannot pass for itself."
5Analytics/hr/separations/analytics: attrition by reason, unit and tenure.
Separation is on the workflow engine for its approval, but the MD's signature and Internal Audit's review are read off the record's named parties, not off a permission — granting HR.Separation.Admin to anyone never confers either. The settlement's money events are registered for the Finance posting sweep rather than posted from HR.

10. Policies and announcements

hr.head
1Announcements/hr/announcements: three live — the Annual Staff Durbar (pinned), revised working hours during the harbour road works, medical screening week — each with an audience rule (all staff, a unit, a location, with exclusions) and an audience preview count before publishing.
2Policy library/hr/policies: Code of Conduct and Ethics v3.1 and the SHE Policy v2.0 (both require acknowledgement, within 30 and 14 days) and the Leave Policy — all three Draft. If the attach is refused with FILE_VIRUS_SCAN_INCOMPLETE, the document scanner is not running — Book 0 §3.2, one command, then retry. This is the one step in the books that depends on it.
3The employee's sidestaff: Time, Leave & Pay → Company Policies · /me/policies: the Code of Conduct is now outstanding for her, due in 30 days. Open it, read the document, Acknowledge — the acknowledgement text is shown and her acceptance is recorded with the version. Back in hr.head, the policy's compliance view shows one acknowledged, the rest outstanding.
