## Result convention

Use only these campaign statuses:

- `Not started` — no representative live run has begun.
- `In progress` — live execution has begun but one or more gates remain.
- `Passed` — all positive, negative, audit and reconciliation gates have evidence.
- `Failed` — an observed result differs from an expected result and a defect is recorded.
- `Blocked` — a prerequisite, environment issue or required business decision prevents execution.
- `Not run — scope decision` — permitted only for an explicitly approved optional child route; it is never equivalent to `Passed`.

For every flow, record the new business reference, date/time, maker and checker accounts, screenshots or exports, API result or `ProblemDetails`, workflow/audit reference, Finance or SQL reconciliation where applicable, and any defect ID. A screen rendering alone is not proof of a workflow, commitment, audit, authorization, idempotency or tenant-isolation result.

## Flow acceptance gates

### `PROC-UAT-F01` — Procurement budget and plan

- Create a current-year departmental `GHS` budget using controlled dimensions, then submit and independently approve it.
- Prove version and workflow history, self-approval denial, distinguishable approved/committed/spent/available values, and no use of Draft, expired or superseded budgets.
- Create a plan with at least two Goods items, verify totals and controlled budget lineage, then independently approve it and verify filter/action eligibility.
- Capture budget/plan references, workflow versions, maker/checker actors, availability values and audit evidence.

### `PROC-UAT-F02` — Manual GHANEPS exchange

- Export the approved APP version in a configured format and verify its system-generated SHA-256 checksum.
- Record submission, acknowledgement, a failed attempt, retry and reconciliation without duplicating the exchange.
- Prove source/package lineage, accepted/rejected information, exception history and resulting register status.
- Do not expect a direct GHANEPS provider API; this campaign tests the governed manual exchange.

### `PROC-UAT-F03` — Purchase requisition

- Create a fresh PR from approved compatible plan items and verify plan, budget, department, cost-centre, category and currency lineage.
- Capture description, positive line specifications, quantity, estimate, required date, justification and controlled supporting documents.
- Prove mandatory validation, budget availability, independent approval, rejection/negative paths, audit history and idempotent replay.
- PR approval checks availability; it must not be recorded as the formal commitment event.

### `PROC-UAT-F04` — Sourcing release and sourcing case

- Release only the approved and complete PR; prove the immutable release audit and policy/version/category/amount/currency/method rationale.
- Create a sourcing case linked to the PR for this campaign, even where direct PR sourcing is supported.
- Prove controlled method selection, shortlist/documents/status history and rejection of an unsupported manual override.
- If a configured exception permits an override, retain its reason, evidence, workflow and approver lineage.

### `PROC-UAT-F05-A` — Standard RFQ

- Create the RFQ from the approved PR, invite the policy-required eligible suppliers and complete dispatch, quotation, opening, evaluation and recommendation.
- Prove commercial confidentiality before opening, supplier eligibility, distinct evaluator/approver actors, one award and idempotent retries.
- Capture the PR, release, sourcing case, RFQ, quotation, evaluation, award and audit references.

### `PROC-UAT-F05-B` — Standard NCT/ICT tender

- Create and approve the tender with schedule, evaluation template, controlled documents and submission deadline, then publish it before any evaluation-committee constitution.
- Receive on-time sealed bids; only then constitute and activate the committee so each member can accept their appointment and submit a current COI declaration. After the submission deadline closes, create the evaluation meeting, sign attendance and confirm quorum before formal bid opening.
- Prove sealed/late-access controls, post-deadline quorum before opening, technical-before-financial sequencing, evaluation, recommendation, independent award and bidder communications.
- Capture tender publication/control, sealed-bid receipts, committee snapshot and appointments, acceptance/COI/activation evidence, meeting/attendance/quorum evidence, opening register, scores, recommendation, approval, award, communication and audit references.

### `PROC-UAT-F06` — Petty Purchase

- Use the effective policy's Petty Purchase classification and the shared sourcing/tender exception controls.
- Prove threshold classification, single eligible supplier, required `DEC-005` evidence, separate approval and immutable justification.
- Confirm a PO and commitment are produced unless an explicitly approved architecture exception authorizes another outcome; `FR-PR-004` requires a PO for procurement purchases.
- Capture the governing policy/version, threshold result, evidence, approval, award/source and audit references.

### `PROC-UAT-F07` — QBS

- Use the controlled tender lifecycle with approved criteria, eligible committee members, COI declarations and signed/quorate scoring.
- Prove that only the unique highest-ranked qualified technical bidder proceeds to the permitted financial opening or negotiation.
- Reject a lower-ranked recommendation unless a configured governed exception exists.
- Capture technical scores, ranking, permitted financial record, recommendation, independent award and audit lineage.

### `PROC-UAT-F08` — QCBS

- Use approved technical/financial weights through the authoritative tender Controls lifecycle.
- Prove technical-first opening, controlled financial opening, server-calculated combined score/ranking and rejection or routing of an unresolved tie.
- Prove a client-selected non-winning recommendation cannot replace the server-calculated winner.
- Capture both score families, weights, combined result, recommendation, independent award and audit lineage.

### `PROC-UAT-F09` — Emergency procurement

- Create a fresh eligible emergency record with timestamped justification, policy rule and controlled evidence.
- Prove independent Internal Audit submission/vouching and configured executive or Board approval before trigger.
- Complete the linked exceptional-sourcing/post-award filing and prove direct status manipulation is rejected.
- Capture emergency, PR, policy, Audit, approval, trigger, tender/award, post-award and exception-register references.

### `PROC-UAT-F10` — Purchase order and commitment

- Create the PO only from the approved governed source and prove budget, supplier, compliance, authority and segregation readiness.
- Prove self-approval denial and that final independent approval creates exactly one formal commitment keyed to the PO.
- Reconcile the PO value, active reservation envelope, commitment register and exact reduction in available budget; retries must not post again.
- Prove insufficient funds or excessive cumulative exposure blocks approval without changing workflow, reservation or commitment data.

### `PROC-UAT-F11` — Contract activation

- Create the contract from the approved supplier/PR/sourcing/award lineage with value, project, approval/start/end dates, retention and required evidence.
- Prove independent approval and activation, exactly-once commitment/budget effect, contract-register visibility and immutable history.
- Prove activation is blocked for missing prerequisites, inactive/ineligible supplier, stale source, failed budget or incompatible actor.
- Record the contract, activation, commitment, workflow, register/report and audit references. Stop the campaign before any receipt or certificate action.

## Cross-cutting gates applied to every flow

| Gate | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Authorization | Allowed actor succeeds; unauthorized actor receives the governed denial without data leakage. |
| Tenant isolation | Alternate-tenant reads and mutations return the governed `403`/`404` result. |
| Maker-checker/SOD | Incompatible stages use distinct real users; self-approval or evaluator/award approval is rejected. |
| Validation and data integrity | Mandatory data and references are enforced; no orphaned or partial record remains after failure. |
| Idempotency and concurrency | Retry or competing final action produces one durable business outcome. |
| Workflow and audit | Create, submit, approve, reject, post, cancel, retry and exception actions retain actor, time and source lineage. |
| Errors | The UI preserves user state and shows an actionable server detail/code; capture the API `ProblemDetails` where applicable. |
| Reports and export | Relevant register/report agrees with the source transaction and governed export. |
| Finance reconciliation | Budget, reservation, commitment and any posting values agree exactly and occur at the correct lifecycle point. |

## Architecture clarifications and campaign decisions

| Topic | Campaign treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Approval thresholds and exact routes | The architecture leaves the approval matrix and thresholds configurable. Record the published tenant policy/workflow used; never invent a threshold or mandatory approver. |
| Sourcing-case requirement | Existing specifications allow direct PR sourcing in some configurations, while implementation readiness notes can require a current case. Because this campaign explicitly includes the case, create one and record the actual configured route. |
| Commitment timing | Treat approved PO or contract activation as the formal commitment point, consistent with `FR-BG-009` and `FR-PR-005`; PR approval is an availability check only. |
| RFQ and tender coverage | Run both F05-A and F05-B for full standard-route coverage. A business-approved scope exclusion must remain visibly not run. |
| Petty/QBS/QCBS/emergency detail | These methods are not defined at state-machine level in the architecture document. Use the approved effective policy plus the canonical UAT and current controlled implementation; flag any business-rule mismatch as a design defect. |
| Contract activation | The architecture defines the contract register and monitoring but not explicit activation states. Use the current governed activation workflow and record all prerequisites and state transitions. |
| GHANEPS | Test controlled manual file exchange only until an approved provider interface contract exists. |

## Procurement-to-Inventory exit gate

Inventory testing may begin only when all of the following are true:

- F01 through F11 have a terminal campaign result and every planned positive and negative gate has evidence.
- Both F05-A and F05-B passed, or an approved scope exclusion is recorded without misrepresenting coverage.
- No open Critical or High defect can corrupt procurement lineage, budget, commitment, supplier eligibility, authorization or audit history.
- PO and contract commitments reconcile exactly to the approved budget and registers.
- Permissions, tenant isolation, maker-checker, retry/idempotency, workflow/audit and relevant report/export evidence are complete.
- Phase 4 acceptance remains open until the representative `E2E-003` Works-procurement UAT is executed and its evidence is recorded in the implementation tracker.
- The campaign closes with an explicit decision to hand the approved PO/contract boundary into [Inventory and Stores UAT](INVENTORY_STORES_END_TO_END_UAT.md); receipt and stock actions are not backfilled into this tracker.
