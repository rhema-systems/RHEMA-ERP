# Customer accounts and Procurement invoice ownership

This follow-up addresses the missing accounting-book period schema, customer-only withholding visibility, the three omitted GP customer adjustment accounts, and the original requirement for a Procurement-owned supplier AP invoice workspace. Supplier invoices are AP; the AR invoice created during customer verification is a separate customer transaction.

## Implemented scope

- Customer-only create/edit forms hide supplier WHT rate and withholding-deduction controls while retaining their saved values. Supplier, contractor and dual-role records retain those controls. Customer tax schedules remain available.
- Customer mappings now include Finance Charges (Revenue), Writeoffs (Expense), and Overpayment Writeoffs (Revenue), separately from supplier mappings. The existing AR adjustment workflow consumes these defaults, with eligible explicit contra accounts taking precedence. Finance charges debit AR; writeoffs credit AR; overpayment writeoffs debit an AR credit balance. These are functional-currency customer balance adjustments, not automatic invoice settlement or customer-advance disposal. They retain existing adjustment permissions and the central posting engine's book/period controls.
- Adjustment requests carry stable IDs. Replays retain the posted result and reject changed requests. New adjustments capture their control and contra accounts; reversals use captured accounts after customer default changes. Balance limits and serializable database transactions protect customer writeoffs. AR reporting includes both an original reversed adjustment and its compensating adjustment.
- Procurement owns `/procurement/supplier-invoices`, including create/detail/edit pages. These files do not import Finance page implementations. The shared AP service accepts a separate invoice API base while retaining its existing default for Finance.
- `/api/procurement/supplier-invoices` reuses the existing AP controller, permissions, matching, approval, SOD, dimensions and posting engine. A route boundary forces Procurement-only queries, checks same-tenant PO/accepted-supply/auto-invoice/landed-cost lineage for reads and mutations, rejects manual/opening creation, and prevents removing source ownership on update. Finance's existing route remains available unchanged.
