# Workmen's Compensation Act 1987 (PNDCL 187) — the provisions and schedules HR relies on

> **Verified 2026-09-26 against the primary text** (round 5, lane K-II-0): the Parliament of Ghana's
> revised edition, *PNDCL 187 Rev Ed.pdf*, pages VII-4451 to VII-4476 [Issue 1], from
> `repository.parliament.gh` (item *Workmen's Compensation ACT, 1987 PNDCL 187 (Revised Edition)*).
> The file is a scan with no text layer; it was read page by page. Made 12 August 1987, notified in
> the *Gazette* on 21 August 1987. **This catalogue is the seed source for lane K-II-b** — every
> figure below is the Act's, and every one becomes an editable default, never a constant.
>
> ⚠ **A secondary copy (ghanalegal.com) was wrong in three places** the planning drew on: it put the
> statutory medical board in section 8 (it is in the First Schedule's note, for disfigurement
> disputes only), put the "50 % for partial loss of use" rule in section 6 (it is in the Third
> Schedule's notes), and gave loss of an arm at the shoulder as 80 % (it is 100 %; 80 % is between
> elbow and shoulder). Use this file, not that one.

## The statutory medical boards

Two, both in the schedules' notes — neither is a general incapacity tribunal:

1. **First Schedule note (disfigurement).** Where there is a dispute whether there has been a
   conspicuous deformity, or functional loss of genital organs, "the doubt shall be resolved by a
   medical board **appointed by the chief labour officer**", consisting of a medical practitioner as
   chairman nominated by the **Minister**, a medical practitioner nominated by the **employer**, and,
   if the employee wishes, a medical practitioner nominated by the **employee**.
2. **Third Schedule note (internal organs).** "Where there is loss of, or injury to, an internal organ
   such as the spleen, kidney, and others and the spine, not resulting in total incapacity, **a medical
   board appointed by the Minister** and the rate of compensation payable shall not exceed the
   compensation payable in respect of partial incapacity."

An employer's own board (TDC's) is none of these; the system records which kind sat.

**The Schedule's own rules**, verbatim in substance:

- Loss of, or injury to, an internal organ (spleen, kidney, others, the spine) not resulting in total
  incapacity: a medical board appointed by the Minister; not more than partial incapacity.
- **Total permanent loss of the use of a member shall be treated as loss of the member.**
- **Permanent partial loss of the use of a member: rated at fifty percent** of the percentage for total
  permanent loss of the member.
- **A right-handed employee's injury to the left arm or hand, or a left-handed employee's to the right,
  is rated at ninety percent** of the percentage above.
- **Loss of two or more parts of the hand: not more than for the loss of the whole hand.**
