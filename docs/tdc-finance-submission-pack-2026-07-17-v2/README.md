## Status Interpretation

`Implemented - Pending UAT` means a Finance technical foundation exists but still needs TDC configuration, business scenarios, and accountant/UAT evidence. It does **not** mean production go-live approved.

`Blocked - Dependency` and `Not Finance Owned - Interface` deliberately identify the responsible external module, system, or business decision. They are not Finance delivery claims.

## Production Readiness Boundary

This pack does not authorize production migration or go-live. `FIN-LIM-0017` remains open until real representative cutover data and accountant-reviewed evidence exist. `FIN-LIM-0048` remains open globally unless the real cutover has no source-level AP/AR/fixed-asset opening requirement or those openings are loaded through proper source-document/import paths.
